Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export and publish native APKs and Google Play AABs?

+
Yes! CreSuit compiles your visual logic into standard Android package files (`.apk`) for sideloading or direct installation, as well as signed Android App Bundles (`.aab`) ready for submission to the Google Play Store.

How does the generative AI prompt-to-app workflow work?

+
Our multi-model engine analyzes your plain English prompt, synthesizes the complete screen layout (SCM), generates the corresponding event-driven visual logic blocks (BKY), and binds them to AI and CloudDB components automatically.

What is the CreSuit Companion APK?

+
The CreSuit Companion is a live testing client for your phone. When connected over WiFi via a 6-character code or QR scan, your app's screens and logic update on your physical device in real time with zero packaging delay.

Which AI models are supported out of the box?

+
CreSuit includes native components for OpenAI (GPT-4o, DALL-E), Google Gemini 1.5, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Image Classification (Teachable Machine), and custom REST endpoint proxies.
